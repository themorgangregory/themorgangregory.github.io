    <p>Morgan Gregory (b. Richmond, Virginia) is a performer/choreographer/… who focuses on reimagining Afro-diasporic imagery through a futurist lens. In her choreographic and performance work development, she constantly explores ancestral importance and a nostalgic taste for the American South, and in this continuous effort of understanding, she dreams within intersectional identities. She is a graduate of the Ailey/Fordham BFA program, honored as a Denise Jefferson Memorial Scholar.</p>
